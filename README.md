About Ganzo
Ganzo is an Agentic Blockchain with Proof of Useful Inference (PoUI). It leverages PQ-ZK to make AI computation secure, verifiable, private, and quantum resistant. The Ganzo Agentic Blockchain gives any Agentic Device a way to connect, exchange, and train intelligence.

Inference & Training
Providers will use the agentic blockchain to provide Inference from local GPUs or remote compute. Multi-Agents manage agents, inference engines, LLMs, and training across the network.
