About Ganzo

Ganzo is an Agentic Blockchain with Proof of Useful Inference (PoUI). It leverages PQ-ZK to make AI computation secure, verifiable, private, and quantum resistant. The Ganzo Agentic Blockchain gives any Agentic Device a way to connect, exchange, and train intelligence.

Inference & Training

Providers will use the agentic blockchain to provide Inference from local GPUs or remote compute. Multi-Agents manage agents, inference engines, LLMs, and training across the network.

FAQ

What is Ganzo?

An agentic blockchain for verifiable AI inference, training, private agents, and quantum-resistant compute.

What is PoUI?

Proof of Useful Inference makes AI inference and training work useful, verifiable, and economically aligned.

What is PoA?

Proof of Authority is the Ganzo Blockchain consensus model. Every Ganzo App is a validator, a distributed validator consensus. Quantum signatures and PQ-ZK proofs guarantee correctness with instant finality.

What is PQ-ZK?

Post Quantum Zero Knowledge helps verify Inference and Training work without exposing unnecessary private data.

Can Ganzo run on local devices?

Yes. Ganzo is designed for GPUs, computers, smartphones, edge devices, robots, and other agentic devices.

Does Ganzo collect data?

No. Ganzo has Zero Data Retention policy. Your data is encrypted and accessible only by you. When inference is performed using Ganzo models collection, all data is private.
